# Student-Dropout-Prediction-System
In most of the universities in Malaysia, student performance issues such as academic failure and dropout are often identified too late, when little can be done to intervene effectively. Current Student Information Systems (SIS) mainly serve as record-keeping tools and do not provide the predictive insights needed to support timely decision-making. This results in missed opportunities to assist struggling students and improve overall educational outcomes.

This project proposes an Analytics-based Student Performance Prediction System that leverages machine learning to analyze academic, lifestyle and behavioral data in order to predict student performance and dropout risks. The system will generate actionable insights through dashboards and reports, enabling educators and administrators to identify at-risk students early and take proactive measures.

The opportunity lies in transforming existing SIS platforms into intelligent decision-support systems, helping universities reduce dropout rates, improve student retention, and enhance the overall quality of education. The primary stakeholders include students (who benefit from personalized feedback), teachers and advisors (who can identify and support struggling learners), and university administrators (who gain institution-wide insights for policy and resource allocation).
